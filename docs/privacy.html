Remember — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Remember is an invite-only app for collecting photos and videos from private events and turning them into a shared film. This policy explains what data the app stores and why.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and display name, used for passwordless sign-in and invitations.
  • Media: photos, videos and audio you upload to a memory, together with timestamps and optional location metadata attached by your device.
  • Contacts: contact suggestions from your address book, used only to invite people you choose.
  • Device data: push notification tokens and basic app diagnostics needed to deliver notifications and keep the service working.

How the data is used

Media is stored in our private object storage and shown only to members of the memory you uploaded it to. When you create a film, selected media is sent to a third-party AI provider to generate the montage. Email delivery and push notifications are handled by external providers.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. Other members can see only the memories they were invited to. Service providers process data solely to operate Remember.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account and memories are active. To request deletion of your account or media, contact us at the address below.
